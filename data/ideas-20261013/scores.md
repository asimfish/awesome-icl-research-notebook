C15, C21 and C12 receive **revise**, in that order, for bounded mechanism clarification and closest-prior reading. The other 37 are rejected **as the current algorithm proposal**, not as useful baselines or worthwhile research problems. None is currently recommended for an efficacy pilot. A ranking expresses refinement priority, not evidence of novelty or expected publication acceptance.

This fresh review read all 40 entries in `candidates.md`, every entry and search annotation in `CANDIDATE_POOL.json`, the research brief, problem anchor, gaps, complete registry, and all 15 results-masked method digests. It did not browse, inspect newly returned paper bodies, run experiments, train a model, or perform an independent/cross-model review. The new search hits are proximity signals only; their exact coverage remains unresolved. Local numeric references below refer to `paper-registry.json`, not Svalbard IDs. Source mechanisms described in the digests are distinguished from reviewer inferences.

The prior history-retention result establishes utility of history on a development cohort, not reusable relation learning, and the original completion gate failed. Exact Bayes with hierarchical priors already reproduced toy transfer. Therefore a further transfer toy, a crossed-odds or erasure result, and generic correction propagation do not create algorithmic evidence. Frozen deployment neural parameters and teacher exit are constraints, not contributions; external inference memory is allowed. No hidden failure-cause, predicate, eligibility, dependency-mask, or changed-edge oracle is permitted. ## Candidate verdicts

### C1 verdict
- **Title**: Marginalize the cause before updating the relation.
- **Verdict**: reject(reason=latent-cause Bayes is the entire specified update, while the required consequence likelihood is assumed rather than learned by a new operation).
- **Evidence**: CML [3] already weights persistent hypotheses by likelihood; OHCAM [5] exposes a real success/predicate interface limitation but does not establish a new solution. The proposal explicitly integrates nuisance causes and then performs ordinary inference. The annotated *Probabilistic Failure Isolation for Cognitive Robots* is a direct reading priority if attribution is revived; its body has not been read here. The proposal supplies no calibrated observable mapping that would address the hard part.
- **Strongest objection**: An exact latent-cause Bayesian baseline given the same likelihood implements all three proposed steps.

### C2 verdict
- **Title**: Separate a shared disturbance from a persistent rule change.
- **Verdict**: reject(reason=the new latent variable specifies a model class but no new inference or identification operation).
- **Evidence**: BOCPD [12] already maintains competing growth/reset explanations; CML [3] retains joint state. Sharing a disturbance across commands is meaningful modeling, but robust/multivariate BOCPD are named primary search signals. If transient and persistent hypotheses make the same allowed predictions, the task is unidentified; supplying a true regime label would remove the problem by privilege. An independently learned physical observation family could change this verdict, but it is not specified.
- **Strongest objection**: A joint change-point/disturbance model with exact inference can reproduce the result without a new learning algorithm.

### C3 verdict
- **Title**: Compile ambiguous corrections into joint constraints.
- **Verdict**: reject(reason=weighted action-model version-space compilation is a known operation and no additional compression algorithm is specified).
- **Evidence**: The OHCAM [5] factorization boundary motivates a stronger control, while CML [3] already retains complete alternatives. The pool identifies *Learning Partially Observable Action Models: Efficient Algorithms* and weighted MAX-SAT action learning as close signals. No body-level claim about their exact noisy interface is made. Merely showing that marginal scores admit an impossible conjunction establishes a weak-baseline defect, not superiority to existing joint logical inference.
- **Strongest objection**: Once a weighted logical compiler and the same likelihood factors are supplied, the proposed method has no remaining distinct operation.

### C4 verdict
- **Title**: Score a whole plan within each surviving explanation.
- **Verdict**: reject(reason=whole-plan evaluation under persistent model identity is ordinary Bayesian or robust planning).
- **Evidence**: CML [3] keeps joint ancestry and DICP [2] searches consequence futures. MTSGI [10] policy mixing is useful context but is not the strongest control. The annotated *Planning with Uncertain Action Models* and weighted-model-count planning require later body review if needed. Independent per-step model resampling is an artificial relaxation that cannot establish a new planning method when corrected.
- **Strongest objection**: Exact uncertain-model planning already preserves one common model across a plan and answers the same decision problem.

### C5 verdict
- **Title**: Discover relation variables through valid local substitutions.
- **Verdict**: reject(reason=CoDA plus invariant feature selection covers the specified operation and the local dependency discovery problem remains assumed).
- **Evidence**: CoDA [11] already requires valid original and modified assignments in the same local dependency region. OHCAM [5] does leave a predicate-grounding boundary, but selecting observable features after augmentation does not fill it with a distinct learner. RoCoDA, CAIAC and Act-to-Ground are explicit unread proximity signals. Masks must be inferred from accessible data or honestly supplied to every method as a restricted reference interface.
- **Strongest objection**: The candidate either inherits the hard causal mask or becomes ordinary same-data CoDA followed by feature selection.

### C6 verdict
- **Title**: Split the interface when its decision operators disagree.
- **Verdict**: reject(reason=Bellman-test partition refinement is the known abstraction operation, without a new way to choose or identify its tests).
- **Evidence**: Value equivalence [13] explicitly depends on the considered functions and closure under operators. The new variable is justified only after a separating accessible distinction exists, so grounding and test coverage remain open. *Abstraction Selection in Model-Based Reinforcement Learning* and *Predicate Invention for Bilevel Planning* are close unread signals. A goal omitted from a deliberately weak initial basis is not evidence of an algorithmic advantage over adaptive abstraction.
- **Strongest objection**: Equal-budget abstraction refinement given the same tests can make the same splits.

### C7 verdict
- **Title**: Turn successor-occupancy contrasts into interface coordinates.
- **Verdict**: reject(reason=successor-based representation learning and ordinary occupancy updates implement the stated mechanism).

Expected occupancies can also hide non-additive conjunctions; a low-rank coordinate is not automatically a reusable logical relation.
- **Strongest objection**: The same frozen features and skill rollouts let a standard successor representation learner discover the same occupancy distinctions.

### C8 verdict

Removing expert predicate answers is a valuable interface restriction, not a new test-selection algorithm. The pool names *Spectral Learning of Predictive State Representations with Insufficient Statistics* and embodied relational active learning for further reading. Reachability costs must be matched to a control, not omitted there.
- **Strongest objection**: A cost-aware predictive-state test selector can construct the same variables from the same legal outcomes.

### C10 verdict

### C11 verdict
- **Title**: Counterexample-guided memory state splitting.
- **Verdict**: reject(reason=closure under observed successors is automata or predictive-state refinement, and the budgeted merge rule is unspecified).
- **Evidence**: A present-action-only RAD [1] objective need not guarantee next-update sufficiency, but VE [13] and predictive states already make future-test closure explicit. ### C12 verdict
- **Title**: Provisional evidence escrow with bounded replay.
- **Verdict**: revise(focus=specify an observable evidence-dependent checkpoint advancement rule with a measured regret-versus-replay-cost advantage over adaptive fixed-lag smoothing).
- **Evidence**: RAD [1] compresses/overwrites memory and BOCPD [12] retains temporal alternatives, so irreversible compression is a coherent risk, not an observed failure here. Checkpoint plus pending suffix is executable on CPU. A fixed pending buffer is ordinary delayed assimilation; only when to advance its causal boundary remains a separable operation. The pool's out-of-sequence filtering hit has unverified metadata and cannot certify absence of adaptive commitment. A stability score must not silently mean the latent cause is now known.
- **Strongest objection**: Longer fixed-lag retention can preserve at least the same records, so early commitment needs a real compute or storage tradeoff and cannot claim free accuracy gains.

### C13 verdict
- **Title**: Joint filtering of outcomes and their observation process.
- **Verdict**: reject(reason=joint observation-process likelihood and marginalization are standard missing-data/POMDP inference).
- **Evidence**: CML [3] allows intermittent feedback and DICP [2] predicts consequences; neither licenses assuming missing-at-random. The proposed sum over missing outcomes is a correct selection-model likelihood. Its difficult part is identifying an outcome-dependent observation channel from censored data, and the steps do not add information or an identification result. The missingness-aware OPE search hit is a warning, not a claimed exact implementation of this robot updater.
- **Strongest objection**: Correct-interface joint Bayes duplicates the method, while unidentified censoring cannot be resolved by the posterior formula.

### C14 verdict
- **Title**: Likelihood-set updating when censoring is unidentified.
- **Verdict**: reject(reason=credal updating and minimax belief-state control are the specified method).

### C15 verdict
- **Title**: Particle preservation by update-response diversity.
- **Verdict**: revise(focus=define weighted allocation by future evidence-response moments and show a finite-budget advantage beyond stratified resample-move and sequential coresets).
- **Evidence**: CML [3] uses finite particles with ancestry; a population can lose an explanation needed later, but that failure has not been measured here. The pool explicitly names *Sequential core-set Monte Carlo* and *Memory (and Time) Efficient Sequential Monte Carlo*; their bodies must be read, and a generic diverse population is insufficient.
- **Strongest objection**: Response fingerprints may be standard predictive-moment stratification or coreset selection with overhead that erases all decision benefits.

### C21 verdict
