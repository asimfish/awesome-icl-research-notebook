# Three conditional refinements from the complete pool

Disposition: **RETHINK**. C15, C21 and C12 are **REFINE**, in that initial priority order. None survives as a sufficiently distinct algorithm; none is ready for an efficacy pilot. The later body audit and proposal-blind derivation strengthen the overlap concerns. This document specifies the current operations for review, rather than turning a ranking into an acceptance claim.

The complete forty-entry pool remains in `candidates.md` and `CANDIDATE_POOL.json`, with all entries scored in `reviews/shortlist.json`. Forty ideas were useful for coverage: uncertain evidence, joint inference, predictive interfaces, dependence-constrained reuse, model repair, composition interactions, teaching and acquisition. Three receive deeper treatment because they name separable resource decisions: particle allocation, query allocation and chronological checkpoint commitment. The other thirty-seven are rejected as their current algorithm proposals, often because their stated steps reduce to known Bayes, predictive representations, augmentation, planning, supervision or caching. That does not reject their research problems or their use as strong baselines. Three is a refinement limit, not a quota requiring three new algorithms.

This is the adapted report-writer stage. The native HTTP LLM loop, full Svalbard vault and trained adapters were unavailable; numeric citations are local checked registry IDs. The report has results-masked proposal fields and no outcome claims. Prior project experiments remain separate. No new pilot, training, robot execution, public edit or independent-model acceptance is reported here.

## C15 — Response-preserving particle allocation — REFINE

**Current operation.** Preserve the original causal filter and change its resampling allocation according to modeled next-feedback/goal response features. This is an exploratory application of established stratified sampling, not an accepted new algorithm.

**Method steps, before hypotheses:**

1. Maintain the original frozen learned gate, module hidden states, ancestry and causal likelihood update. At a resampling boundary, compute each particle's likelihood for a bounded catalogue of possible legal command consequences and its subsequent goal-action utility under the common frozen planner.
2. Group particles by the joint likelihood and likelihood-times-utility features. Use exact signatures in the finite reference or declared coordinate bins selected on development data. Reserve one output slot per occupied positive-mass group when the capacity permits, and allocate remaining slots with the standard variance rule below.
3. Resample within each group using its original relative weights and give offspring the group's original total mass divided by its allocated count. Copy full causal states. Any posterior-invariant suffix move uses the same standard kernel and cost in all comparators.
4. When the next actual consequence arrives, apply the original causal transition and likelihood update. Answer the public later goal before its execution; recompute the following catalogue only from currently available causal information.

Let the weighted input population be \(E_t=\{(S_i,w_i)\}\), \(\sum_iw_i=1\). A legal modeled next event \(e=(u,y,o')\) includes a command and its observable consequence/post-command observation. With the common frozen model and planner,

\[
L_i(e)=p_\phi(e\mid S_i,H_t,u),\qquad
V_E(g,a\mid e)=\frac{\sum_iw_iL_i(e)Q_i(g,a;e)}{\sum_iw_iL_i(e)}.
\]

The feature vector retains \(L_i(e)\) and \(L_i(e)Q_i(g,a;e)\) for the declared tests. This is the ordinary Bayes ratio. Preserving current actions, \(E[L]\) and \(E[Q]\) separately does not preserve its joint numerator. That observation is already supported by predictive-state/value-equivalence principles [13][20][21] and reconstructed in `reviews/proposal-blind-derivation.json`.

For group \(c\), let \(W_c=\sum_{i\in c}w_i\). With \(C\) occupied groups, reserve \(n_c=1\) and allocate the remaining \(K-C\) slots by largest remainders proportional to

\[
r_c=W_c\sqrt{\sum_f\rho_f\operatorname{Var}_c(f)},\qquad
\sum_cn_c=K.
\]

Feature weights \(\rho_f\) and tie-breaking are fixed before evaluation. If every \(r_c=0\), allocate the remainder by \(W_c\). Sample a parent with probability \(w_i/W_c\) and assign each child weight \(W_c/n_c\). Each group's total output mass is exactly \(W_c\). Conditional on the input population and fixed allocation, the empirical measure is preserved **in expectation**; for independent draws the standard variance of an estimated feature is \(\sum_cW_c^2\operatorname{Var}_c(f)/n_c\). If all retained features are constant within exact groups, their moments and positive-denominator one-event readouts equal those of the input population. None of this establishes an exact true posterior, unbiased general ratios, indefinite preservation or better decisions. No new proof is offered.

**Catalogue and capacity.** Predeclare a design-goal family, observable feedback tokenizer including missing/other outcomes, finite action/readout set and at most \(J\) event entries. Commands must be ordinary legal actions at the currently observed state; neither their applicability nor their consequences come from true latent labels. Hypothetical events are frozen-model computations, never factual evidence. The future realized event and eventual target goal cannot select the catalogue. A goal or event outside it receives no protection claim. With \(K\) input particles, there are at most \(K\) occupied groups; if all signatures differ, the rule can merely retain all original weighted states and does not cure weight degeneracy. With larger augmented support, more groups than output slots makes universal protection impossible; charge the support and report failure rather than hide it.

**Frozen learned deployment.** A CML-compatible comparison must retain its gating RNN and module RNNs: \(\gamma_t=G_\phi(x_t,\gamma_{t-1},z_{t-1})\), a categorical module sample from that learned gate, and \(\mu_t=M_\phi^{z_t}(x_t,\mu_{t-1})\), together with ancestry and target/feedback likelihood [3]. A uniform gate is not CML. Training-only future-guided proposals cannot enter deployment. A lawful robot consequence likelihood and public goal decoder are disclosed common interface adaptations; the paper's target-output likelihood does not supply them. No trained robot instance is available here.

**Exact information boundary.** Deployment receives actual causal observations, executed commands, observed consequences and a public goal description. It receives no true prerequisite, failure cause, eligibility vector, changed edge, dependency mask, future output or restored-branch evidence. Neural parameters stay frozen and the teacher exits. Any privileged offline supervision goes to all learned controls under the same budget. A suffix-only move cannot reconstruct a discarded prefix unless its disclosed kernel can actually change that prefix.

**Closest mechanism papers.** *Stratification and Optimal Resampling for Sequential Monte Carlo* explicitly orders particles by a target integrand [17]. *Optimized Auxiliary Particle Filters* optimizes mixture proposals and corrects weights; ordinary APF can wait for the actual incoming observation [16]. *Preserving Modes and Messages via Diverse Particle Selection* preserves subsequent max-product messages [19]; its maximum differs from this normalized sum-product target. *Sequential core-set Monte Carlo* [22] and *Memory (and Time) Efficient Sequential Monte Carlo* [23] occupy memory/rejuvenation/streaming operations. These sources do not prove an exact robot-system duplicate, but they occupy the broad proposed operations. The proposal-blind derivation independently reconstructs this particular L/LQ, mass-correct stratification from standard ingredients. Its reviewer is the same model family, so the comparison remains provisional rather than an independent novelty judgment.

**Hypothesis and precise cost target.** If a residual operation survives prior comparison, response allocation might reduce the post-feedback first-action regret to exact finite Bayes at fixed total peak bytes \(B\) and inference work \(F\). With \(d=|\mathcal C_t|(1+|\mathcal G_{design}||\mathcal A|)\), ordinary feature storage costs \(O(Kd)\), in addition to complete particle states, ancestry, moves and workspace. Charge up to \(K|\mathcal C_t|\) likelihood predictions, \(K|\mathcal C_t||\mathcal G_{design}||\mathcal A|\) planner readouts, and their underlying gate/model branches. Those costs can erase the benefit. Physical commands include preparation, diagnostics, failures, restoration and resets; modeled branches contribute no extra observed trials.

**Conditional first gate.** First compare the stated operation to [16][17][19][22][23], preserving their real assumptions, and identify a same-information strong-baseline failure. Only if a nontrivial difference remains, use one finite noisy prerequisite/destructive-effect family where current choices agree and a reachable consequence separates later-goal choices. Include a no-disagreement case and a legally reachable withheld event. Retain exact joint Bayes and the common planner as correctness references. Compare ordinary resample-move, generic diversity, target-integrand stratification, direct L/LQ moment selection and applicable auxiliary/coreset controls at equal total bytes and all likelihood/planning work. Every strong comparator gets the exact same L/LQ bank. Compare matched records first; any later own-policy acquisition comparison is a separate estimate.

**Falsification.** Stop the algorithm claim if a standard same-feature operation implements it, if a strong equal-cost comparator matches its regret-cost frontier, if catalogue overhead removes gains, or if protection relies on a true cause, future target feedback or an uncharged archive. In-catalogue particle arithmetic is not relation learning. A later learned study would require actual predeployment training, a held-out relation instance, its own causal correction stream, an unexecuted later goal, reset-memory and irrelevant-goal controls, and same-data full-history/equal-memory RAD/AD, full DICP-IDT/MPC, full-gating CML and multi-goal predictive distillation [1][2][3][13][20][21]. This later stage is conditional, not a present benchmark programme.

## C21 — Bounded product-query insertion — REFINE

**Current operation.** Expand a retained query family when supported models agree on existing tests but disagree on a requested conjunction. The unresolved issue is whether the joint evidence remains recoverable at lower **total** cost than standard adaptive refinement.

**Method steps, before hypotheses:**

1. Declare the query sketch and its accessible support: retained relation hypotheses or actual evidence factors/receipts. Charge models, receipt data, indices, decoder and query-transition metadata to one memory budget; a small cache with a full free archive is not compression.
2. On the public description of an unexecuted conjunction, search the retained support for models that agree on cached tests but disagree on that conjunction. The counterexample is supported-model disagreement, not an observed target outcome or a hidden true relation.
3. Insert the product query and its required evidence-transition closure using only still-accessible support. Replay factual support causally and charge pair search, closure, replay and eviction. If the needed correlation has been discarded, record that the query is unresolved.
4. Plan using the resulting joint predictions and retained uncertainty before executing the target goal. Do not choose a model using future feedback to complete the sketch.

For observable component-goal tests \(q_1,\ldots,q_m\), the requested conjunction can be represented by \(q_\wedge=\prod_jq_j\). Seek supported models \(M,M'\) agreeing on retained tests \(\mathcal Q\) but disagreeing on the common planner's update/readout of \(q_\wedge\). Append that query and the necessary legal evidence-transition tests, within the declared budget. This is adaptive query/basis expansion. The proposal-blind update-closed predictive-state derivation already shows a standard construction: retain a test basis whose span closes under legal feedback operators, with normalized predictive updates. Merely adding the conjunction is not a new principle.

**Known part and closest mechanism papers.** *The Value Equivalence Principle for Model-Based Reinforcement Learning* makes agreement relative to chosen policy/value-function sets and their closure [13]. *Efficient Learning and Planning with Compressed Predictive States* supplies recursively updated observable test predictions [20]. *Counterexample-Guided Cartesian Abstraction Refinement* is a direct named neighbor, but only its primary metadata/abstract were audited in this cycle; exact noisy-belief coverage is unresolved and cannot be inferred from its title. CML retains joint alternatives [3]. No novelty is claimed from combining these mechanisms.

**Hypothesis and precise cost target.** A bounded query allocation might attain the same prefeedback conjunction decisions with fewer total stored bytes and less query/refinement work than a matched adaptive CEGAR/VE method. The current proposal has no explicit representation that resolves its main tension: storing every complete model or raw receipt may consume the full reference cost, while deleting correlation makes later product queries unrecoverable. Pair search can be quadratic in retained support and can cost as much as planning; its actual calls and workspace must be reported. No new hidden archive, decoder or search module is invented here to repair that gap.

**Conditional first gate.** Read the relevant CEGAR body and action-model support representation, then identify a fully charged recoverable representation and a changed operation beyond adaptive query closure. If that exists, use one exact finite family with consumable/destructive effects, increasing independent components and sparse genuine interactions. Compare identical support, public clauses, conjunction labels, query budget and total bytes/work against adaptive CEGAR, expanding multi-goal VE, ordinary hard-query mining and full joint finite inference. Measure first conjunction-decision regret, total bytes and pair/closure/replay work. Do not supply the true sparse interaction graph to choose the cache.

**Falsification.** Stop if standard adaptive refinement has the same operation/cost, if complete model/receipt retention removes the saving, if a discarded correlation needs target feedback or hidden labels to recover, or if equal-query hard-example mining/VE matches the tradeoff. A marginal-versus-joint counterexample is a representation diagnostic, not algorithmic novelty or robotic transfer evidence.

## C12 — Chronological checkpoint commitment with bounded replay — REFINE

**Current operation.** Retain a committed prefix checkpoint and a chronological suffix so later legal evidence can revise pending interpretations without inverting a frozen neural update. The proposed decision is when to advance that boundary; data-dependent stopping is already established.

**Method steps, before hypotheses:**

1. Store a committed causal-prefix checkpoint \(z_b\) and pending chronological suffix \(E_{b+1:t}\), with event identifiers and all interpretive support included in a fixed byte budget. Predict by replaying the suffix from the checkpoint with the common frozen updater.
2. Evaluate a declared commitment score for each permissible oldest-first boundary using current legal evidence and modeled still-supported delayed observations. A possible score is downstream decision change between retaining an ambiguous interpretation and its proposed compression; its estimator, horizon and calibration remain unresolved.
3. Advance only a chronological causal boundary. Replay pending events when newly available own evidence changes their interpretation, without counting an event twice. At capacity, an explicit forced-eviction rule must expose irreversible loss; one old retained event does not permit skipping intervening state-changing commands.
4. Answer the later unexecuted goal from the resulting state. Charge score computation, checkpoint reconstruction, support storage and every replay; report unresolved histories and premature commitments.

The state has the form \(m_t=(z_b,E_{b+1:t})\) with prediction state \(U_\phi(z_b,E_{b+1:t})\). A commitment rule would choose \(b'>b\) only after a declared risk estimate \(R_t(b')\) satisfies a fixed tolerance; forced eviction when no boundary passes is an approximation, not a stability guarantee. No particular calibrated \(R_t\), universally valid delay bound or error-control theorem is available in the current proposal. This notation makes the missing operation explicit rather than presenting a generic confidence threshold as a solved method.

**Known part and closest mechanism paper.** *Particle-based adaptive-lag online marginal smoothing* propagates estimators and stops updating a marginal when a conditional/sample variance criterion falls below tolerance [18]. Its forgetting/mixing assumptions and asymptotic resource statements are not arbitrary hard-byte guarantees. It already occupies adaptive stable stopping. RAD provides irreversible recurrent compression [1]; BOCPD retains alternative temporal explanations [12]; replay and fixed-lag/out-of-sequence assimilation are established. The annotated OpenReview delay-filtering item's exact title/method could not be verified; it remains an unresolved comparison, not a source proving or disproving this rule. Statistical ambiguity resolved later and packets arriving out of sequence are different interfaces, but that distinction does not certify novelty.

**Hypothesis and precise cost target.** A specified decision-sensitive commit rule might reduce total replay/likelihood work at equal peak bytes and prefeedback decision regret compared with tuned adaptive-lag smoothing. Peak bytes include the checkpoint, chronological suffix, event IDs, hypotheses/ancestry and workspace. Cumulative work includes every commitment evaluation and suffix reconstruction. Unresolved evidence cannot quietly create an unbounded buffer. In an exact finite filter with an already sufficient checkpoint, raw-event eviction may lose nothing; any failure must be shown in the actual approximate representation instead of assumed.

**Conditional first gate.** Specify the observable score, causal replay semantics and forced eviction, then compare them to [18] and a verified delayed-assimilation method. If a residual operation and strong-baseline failure remain, use one finite noisy observation channel with legally observed variable disambiguation delays, resource-changing commands and some never-disambiguated histories. Compare immediate compression, tuned fixed-window/fixed-lag replay, adaptive-lag variance stopping and applicable out-of-sequence assimilation at identical causal records, model, peak bytes and replay budget. Exact full-history filtering/smoothing may use only evidence actually available at query time, never future target outcomes.

**Falsification.** Stop if the score is standard adaptive-lag variance/error stopping, if the strong comparator matches its regret-versus-replay frontier, if savings require knowledge of the final disambiguation time or true cause, or if wrong forced commitments/suffix growth violate the declared budget. No guaranteed stability is justified under unrestricted future evidence.
