**C15: particle preservation by update-response diversity.** The useful target is the next-feedback goal decision, whose value depends on E[LQ]/E[L]. The numerator is a joint moment; separately retaining current actions, E[L], and E[Q] need not preserve it. This is a Bayesian identity, not a new theorem or algorithm. The frozen second mechanism already (1) keeps the causal model, learned gate, hidden states and ancestry; (2) computes L and post-feedback LQ signatures; (3) forms exact strata or declared approximate clusters within K; (4) samples inside each stratum with w_i/W_c; (5) assigns offspring weights W_c/n_c; (6) allocates remaining particles by a standard within-stratum variance rule; and (7) updates on actual feedback. The original candidate's first two steps match this mechanism. Its suffix-move step is absent from that frozen derivation, but posterior-invariant resample-move is established and the candidate supplies no new move kernel. A suffix-only move cannot revive a discarded prefix.

The supplied body audit of [Stratification and Optimal Resampling for Sequential Monte Carlo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.01975), §§2.3–2.4 and 3, is the strongest direct operation comparison: it explicitly allows target-integrand ordering and alternative resampling losses. Give this baseline the same L/LQ features. The audit of [Optimized Auxiliary Particle Filters](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v161/branchini21a/branchini21a.pdf), §§2.2 and 3, adds corrected mixture proposals and observation-aware selection. Permit that baseline to delay selection until actual feedback whenever the same memory/timing contract allows it; forcing eager destructive resampling would manufacture a failure. [Preserving Modes and Messages via Diverse Particle Selection](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v32/pacheco14.pdf), §§2–3, already selects for downstream message accuracy, although its max-product operation is not mass-preserving Bayesian sum-product filtering. This distinction limits an identity claim; it does not establish novelty by itself. Sequential coresets and ordinary resample-move remain additional resource-matched controls.

A conditional **baseline-discovery protocol** is useful before a new-method pilot: freeze a causal finite model or an auditable existing trace slice, the public later-goal/readout, the allowed information at each time, and total byte/call/replay budgets; instantiate exact finite Bayes plus the operation-matched established baselines above; then identify whether any practically consequential failure remains under those constraints. This protocol has no novel-method arm. First inspect existing legal traces for the proposed failure; old utility numbers are not substitutes. A finite reference diagnosis may justify further specification, but is neither robot validation nor acceptance of a new algorithm.

An efficacy pilot becomes justified only after (1) the changed operation is fully specified, (2) direct-prior comparison leaves a consequential residual difference, (3) the strongest allowed-information baseline actually exhibits the relevant failure or resource bottleneck, and (4) a frozen comparison charges the same records, labels, likelihood/model calls, catalogues, particle state, receipt archives, replay, fallback and physical actions. Predeclare decision regret to exact Bayes and the total-resource frontier, with no-disagreement, rare/noisy feedback, incomplete-coverage and irrelevant-goal controls where relevant. A later robot-learning claim additionally needs a valid own-feedback likelihood, real predeployment training, frozen deployment weights, held-out relation instances and the first decision on an unexecuted goal.
